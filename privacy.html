Pipal Swastha Sewa – Privacy Policy & User Consent Agreement

Effective Date: 2026-05-19

Pipal Swastha Sewa (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to ensuring that your personal and medical information remains fully under your control.

By using this application, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the terms outlined below.

1. Core Privacy Principle

Pipal Swastha Sewa is designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or process any personal or medical data on our servers.
  • All information entered into the app is stored locally on your device only.
  • We have no access to your personal, health, or emergency contact information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app allows you to store the following information locally on your device:

  • Personal details (name, date of birth, etc.)
  • Emergency contacts
  • Medical conditions, medications, allergies, blood type, and related medical data.

Important: This data never leaves your device. We cannot see, access, or retrieve it.

3. Data Ownership & Control

You are the sole owner of your data.

  • You can add, edit, or delete your information at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all stored data from your device.
  • We do not create backups of your data on any server or cloud service.

4. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third parties — because we simply do not have access to any user data.

  • No analytics tools track your personal or health information.
  • No advertising networks are integrated into the app.
  • No data brokers or partners receive any information from us.

5. App Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions:

  • Storage: To save and retrieve your health data locally on the device.
  • Camera (optional): Only if you choose to capture or attach photos to your records.
  • Phone/Dialer (optional): To allow one-tap emergency calling from saved contacts.

These permissions are used solely to enhance your experience and never to collect or transmit data externally.

6. Data Security

While we do not store your data on any server, we encourage you to:

  • Use a device passcode, fingerprint, or face lock to protect access to your phone.
  • Keep your device software up to date.
  • Be cautious when sharing your device with others.

Since all data is stored locally, the security of your information depends on the security of your device.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has used the app without consent, they may uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

8. User Consent

By downloading, installing, and using Pipal Swastha Sewa, you consent to:

  • Storing your personal and medical information locally on your device.
  • The terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.
  • Taking full responsibility for the accuracy and security of your own data.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
